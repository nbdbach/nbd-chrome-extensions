NBD Auto Refresh

Automatically reload the current tab on a schedule you choose.

Turn it on for the tab you are watching — a dashboard, a build, a queue, a live score — and every other tab is left alone. Pick a cadence from 30 seconds to an hour, watch the countdown, turn it off when you are done.

Why this one

  • No permission warnings. It asks for alarms and storage. That is the whole list. It never requests permission to read your browsing history, and it cannot read the content of any page.
  • No network requests. No analytics, no telemetry, no remote configuration, no external fonts.
  • Per tab, not global. Enabling it on one tab never touches another.
  • Small. Under 20KB of readable, unminified JavaScript.
  • Open source, MIT. Every release is built from a public tag, with the exact submitted package attached to it.

One caveat worth knowing: reloading a page discards anything you have typed into it. The extension tells you so the first time you turn it on.
